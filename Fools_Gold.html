Rules of the Game

🎯 Invest Turns = Choose Your Own Odds (a 9-Sided Die)

  • Every item has a 1-Turn floor — the more Turns you invest in ONE item, the better its odds of coming up Real. You pick this right on the item's detail screen, before you Steal it.
  • Green (400k): 1 Turn → 3–9 Real · 2 Turns (max) → 2–9 Real
  • Gold (700k): 1 Turn → 6–9 Real · 2 Turns → 5–9 Real · 3 Turns (max) → 4–9 Real
  • Red (1500k, doubles to 3000k in Stages 4-5): 1 Turn → 9–9 Real · 2 Turns → 8–9 Real · 3 Turns (max) → 7–9 Real — its ceiling stays well below Green/Gold even at full investment, so it's always the high-stakes bet.
  • ⚠️ Only paying the 1-Turn floor without investing more leaves Gold/Red's odds of Real below 50% — a fine way to lose money if you're not careful!

Real Items

  • Stash it to complete a matching set of 3 → sell all three for 2x profit
  • Or sell it right away for instant cash

Fake Items

  • Ditch it — you lose that item's value outright
  • Or Scam to try and win it back

🎭 Scamming

  • Holding a Green/Gold treasure as collateral → 90% success rate: get back 60% (Green) / 40% (Gold) / 20% (Red) of the value of the item you're scamming; you always lose one random collateral treasure from your Stash, whether you succeed or not.
  • No collateral on hand → a straight 50/50 gamble: win 100% of the value (Green) / 80% (Gold) / 85% (Red); lose and you're out 2x (Green/Gold) or 1.3x (Red) that value.

🎒 The Stash

  • Holds only 2 items at a time
  • Once it's full, swap an old item for the new one, or just sell the new item outright
  • Tap any stashed item at any time to sell it at its current price

🕊️ The No-Scam Path

  • Never Scam once during the entire run (only Ditch fakes) → you can still reach 3★, just at a lower bar (9750k instead of 13000k)
  • A single Scam anywhere in the run closes off this path for that run

🗺️ The 5 Heists

  • Abandoned Church → Merchant Guild → Royal Museum → Noble Treasury → King's Vault
  • Cost to Steal: a -1 Turn floor for every color, with the option to invest more to raise your odds of Real (see "Invest Turns" above). Leaving a room costs nothing.
  • ⚠️ You only get 25 Turns for ALL 5 heists combined — spend them wisely!
  • Out of Turns? Bribe the guard for 1 more — it gets cheaper in later stages, but pricier the more times you bribe within a single run. Or just end the run there.
  • 🎯 Goal: complete all 5 heists and walk away with as much loot as possible.
